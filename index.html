How Our Hot Dog Catering Works

  1. Request a quote. Use our quote form, or download the quote sheet, enter the number of orders for each item (it totals for you) and send it back with your guest count and event city.

Hot Dog Catering FAQ

How much space does the hot dog cart need?

Our N.Y.-style hot dog cart needs a clearance of 5 feet wide to fit through. If you don't have the space, we can bring a smaller portable unit that fits in almost any backyard.

Is there a minimum to bring the hot dog cart?

Yes. There is a 100 hot dog minimum to bring the cart.

Can you cater at a park with no electricity?

Yes. We can provide a generator for an additional fee. Items that need power are shave ice, popcorn and churros.

What's included with catering?

We provide all paper goods, utensils, condiments and staff to serve for 2 hours. Condiments include ketchup, mustard, gourmet mustards, Tapatío, jalapeños, onions, relish, mayo and sauerkraut.

What comes on your burgers?

Our burgers come with lettuce, tomato, pickle, red onion and our house sauce.

Is there a minimum order to cater?

Our minimum depends on the city your event is in. Tell us your city and we'll go over it with you.

Which areas do you serve?

We're based in Upland and our main area is San Bernardino County, including Upland, Rancho Cucamonga, Ontario, Montclair, Chino and Fontana. We also serve nearby Claremont and Pomona, and we cater in Riverside County and Orange County.

How do I get a quote?

Fill out our quote request form, or download our quote sheet, enter the number of orders for each item (it totals for you) and send it back with your guest count and event city. You can also call or text Mike at (909) 291-5214.
